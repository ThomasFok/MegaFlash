# MegaFlash PCB

Currently, there are three PCB revisions, 1.0A, 1.1B and 1.2. They are functionally identical. 

Rev 1.0A board is more compact. It is optimized for machine assembly. 

![Image of MegaFlash PCB Rev 1.0A](/images/megaflash_rev1.0a_s.jpg)

Rev 1.1B board is bigger. It is designed for hand assembly.  The components are either through hole or 1.27mm pitch SOICs. The picture below is Rev 1.1A PCB. The positions of the flash chips and mounting hole are slightly different on Rev 1.1B PCB.

![Image of MegaFlash PCB Rev 1.1A](/images/megaflash_rev1.1a_s.jpg)

Rev 1.2 board replaces the GAL16V8 PLD with three 74HC138.

![Image of MegaFlash PCB Rev 1.2](/images/megaflash_rev1.2_s.jpg)

A brief [PCB Assembly Notes](./pcbassemblynotes.pdf) is available for more information.
